About CodexReset.live

CodexReset.live is an independent developer research project and live radar tracking public usage quota refills on OpenAI Codex and ChatGPT Work.

We monitor official announcements, leader statements (primarily Codex Lead Thibault Sottiaux), and system operational metrics to provide developers with transparent, data-driven reset forecasts and rolling limit countdown timers.

Data is compiled from publicly verified announcements. Not affiliated with or endorsed by OpenAI.

×

Privacy Policy

Zero Personal Data Tracking: We respect developer privacy. CodexReset.live does not require account registration, collect personal identifying information (PII), or log your code.

Local Preferences: Timezone selections, countdown timers, and notification preferences are saved strictly within your browser's local storage and are never uploaded to our servers.

Anonymous Analytics: We use privacy-first aggregated metrics solely to monitor global uptime and improve performance.
