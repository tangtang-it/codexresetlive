1. Mathematical Scoring Formula

The 48-hour probability P(t) is computed as an inspectable weighted sum bounded between [5%, 98%]:

P(t) = Baseline(t) + W_tibo · S_tibo + W_status · S_status + W_cadence · S_cadence + W_milestone · S_milestone - Cooldown(Δt)

Where Baseline(t) is the Poisson arrival baseline (typically 12%), S_tibo represents verified announcement semantic strength, and Cooldown(Δt) exponentially suppresses the likelihood immediately following a confirmed flush.
