07 — Knowledge Base

Frequently asked questions

Did Codex reset today?
This page tracks public reset announcements and links to the source. Its forecast is a heuristic estimate based on public signals and past intervals, not an official prediction or a statistically guaranteed probability. It cannot see whether a reset applies to your account; check your own Usage settings for personal limits.
Is there a public schedule for Codex resets?
OpenAI does not promise a fixed schedule for promotional global or banked resets. Normal usage windows and included limits depend on your plan and account. Check OpenAI Usage settings for your current limits and next refresh.
What is the difference between a global reset, a banked reset, and my personal limit?
A global or automatic reset is an OpenAI offer that may cover selected accounts and limits. A banked reset is stored for later use under the offer terms. Neither guarantees that every account is included. Your normal personal usage windows refresh on their own schedule, shown in Usage settings.
Do banked resets expire?
It depends on the specific offer. Check the expiration and eligible limits shown in your account or the original announcement; receiving a banked reset does not itself refresh your usage.
An announcement says Codex reset, but my limit has not changed. What should I check?
The announcement may cover only selected accounts or limits, and usage can take time to update. Confirm that you are viewing the right account, workspace, and limit in Usage settings, then refresh or reopen the page. Do not redeem or buy another reset just to test the first one.
Can I buy an instant Codex reset?
OpenAI currently offers paid instant resets to some eligible Plus and Pro personal accounts. Check Usage settings to see whether the option is available to you. A purchase applies immediately and can change your next weekly reset date; it is different from usage credits.
How can I keep coding while a limit recovers?
You can use another coding tool or wait for the limit shown in your account to refresh. Other providers have separate plans and usage terms, so check their current limits before relying on them.
